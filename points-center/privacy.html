Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Points Center ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our points and rewards service.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: We collect your user ID and channel product identifier to provide our points tracking and reward services.

Usage Data: We collect information about your interactions with our service, including task completions, check-in history, points transactions, and feature usage.

Device Information: We may collect device type, operating system, and browser information for analytics and service optimization.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Track your points balance and transaction history
- Process daily check-ins and task rewards
- Manage reward exchanges and redemptions
- Improve our services and user experience
- Prevent fraud and abuse

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption (AES-128-CBC) for data transmission and storage. All API communications are signed and verified to ensure data integrity.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with channel partners only as necessary to provide the service (e.g., verifying user identity via channel product identifiers).

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and preferences. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us through our feedback system.

7. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries, please submit a request through our in-app feedback form or contact our support team.
